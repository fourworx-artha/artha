### Data layer (`operations.js`)
- `createAlert` — upsert with `onConflict: 'dedupe_key'`, `ignoreDuplicates: true` → ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING, race-free across two parent devices. **Deviation from blueprint:** the dedupe index is a FULL unique index, not partial — PostgREST's `on_conflict` can't infer a partial index (Postgres requires the predicate, supabase-js can't send it); NULLs are distinct so unlimited no-dedupe alerts still insert.
- `tryCreateAlert` (warn-and-continue wrapper), `getAlerts({ role, memberId })` (parents: target_role parent/all; children: child/all AND addressed-to-them-or-family-wide), `markAlertRead`, `markAllAlertsRead`, `dismissAlert` (sets `dismissed_at` AND `read_at`), `markPayslipPromptAlertsHandled(payslipId)` (retires `payslip_ready`/`payslip_overdue` on settle), `deleteOldAlerts` (>30 days, 24h localStorage guard, called from FamilyContext load).
- Insertion hooks: `approveChoreLog`/`rejectChoreLog` (now `.select().single()` to recover the log row; bell-only `chore_approved`/`chore_rejected` with chore title), `addChoreLog` (`chores_all_done` at LOG time when today's last mandatory chore is logged — pending counts as done, dedupe `alldone:{memberId}:{date}`), `approveRewardRequest` (`reward_approved` banner+bell), `rejectRewardRequest` (bell), `approveSpendingWithdrawal` (`cash_approved` bell, cash destination only).
- `alertFmt()` — fetches family currency for alert copy composed outside React (no `useCurrency` hook in operations).
- Alerts cleared in `importAllData`'s delete phase and Backup's reset (ephemera, never in backups).

## Decisions made
- **Full unique index on `dedupe_key`, not partial** — PostgREST `on_conflict` limitation; behaviourally identical (NULLs distinct).
- **First settle fires `first_payslip` instead of `payslip_settled`** — both at once would double-banner a single event; the summary body rides along on first_payslip so no data is lost.
- **`stage_unlocked` fires in W7** (per the blueprint's W7 insertion-points table) with the W9 copy inline in payslip.js; W9's remaining scope is celebratory banner styling + the graduation flow check.
- **"Shell headers" = Dashboard + child Home headers** — the shells render no header bar; the bell lives on each role's home surface.
- **Dismissing a banner also marks it read** — and settling a payslip retires (read + dismissed) its `payslip_ready`/`payslip_overdue` alerts, extending the handoff note to overdue too.
- **`cash_approved` fires only for cash destination** — bank transfers stay silent per the launch catalog's "these 15, nothing else".
- **Alerts are ephemera** — never in backups, cleared on reset/import, pruned at 30 days.
